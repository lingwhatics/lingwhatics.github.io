A long ride out to the bout de l'île and back along Rivière-des-Prairies. We had a nice picnic along the way. Many new roads and paths along the north edge of the island. The rain caught up to us on our way back so we took the metro from Crémazie to Bonaventure and still got soaked in the last 5 minutes from the station.

Total trip distance: 59.30 km

Moving time: 3h53m
