A beautiful Labour Day with sun and clear skies. We went up to Parc Frédérick Back for a picnic before popping into Jean-Talon for maple syrup and then enjoying ice cream and coffee at Oh My Deer.

I had a few new segments: 
- To the edge of the active quarry from the top of the park,
- To parc Julie-Hamelin, and 
- part of 1ère avenue in Rosemont.

Total trip distance: 34.93 km

Moving time: 2h27m
