I spent the day at the client's office, so I rode my bike because it is the fastest way to get there and lets me easily pickup at daycare afterward. A train was passing through under the Bonaventure Expressway and I had to wait with about 60 other cyclists to get past. After picking up at daycare, I took Fernanda for a ride along the new bit of the St-Antoine bike lane and then back along the old port.

Total trip distance: 10.5 km

Moving time: 0h39m
