Another transit strike Saturday, but with "rush hour" service. However, we didn't use transit because it only ever was available at one end of our trip. First up was flamenco class, then Costco, and finally dinner at Leméac. It was a cool day for riding, but mostly sunny. Dinner was really delicious and with good company.

I rode on a couple new streets up near Laurier on the way back to avoid construction on Parc.

Total trip distance: 21.38 km

Moving time: 1h28m
