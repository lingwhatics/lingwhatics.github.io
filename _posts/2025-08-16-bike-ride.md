We went to the botanical gardens today. It was a nice day at 30 degrees with a clear sky. The ride over was not bad, but the cycleway on Rachel could really use better signal coordination on a Saturday. We had way too many stops at red lights.

The trip back seemed better timed. We even rode on a bit of Coloniale and Sherbrooke where I've never ridden.

Total trip distance: 19.4 km

Moving time: 1h25m
