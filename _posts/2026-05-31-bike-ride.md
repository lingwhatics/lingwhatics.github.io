## Ride 25

Today, we participated in the 41st edition of the Tour de l'île with grandma and one of her friends. It started in Parc Jarry, which was significantly closer to our new house that any of the other years, and ran to NDG, down to the Lachine canal, through old Montreal before taking us all way to the to of the island before bringning us back to Parc Jarry. The weather was threatening rain most of the morning, but it didn't rain, before getting sunny for a few hours in the afternoon.

There were many new roads on the route that I'd never ridden on.

- Total trip distance: 58.96 km
- Moving time: 3h52m
- Bike: cargo bike
