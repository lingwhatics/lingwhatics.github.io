A hot day for fête nationale, so we headed out for a bike ride at 8am when it was already 26 degrees out. We took the Lachine canal path out to Lachine for the first time this year. I had not gone to the end of René-Lévesque Park before today. There are some neat sculptures over there. ON the way home we stopped for an early lunch at Atwater market (Falafel Yoni).

Total trip distance: 34.3 km

Moving time: 2h04m
