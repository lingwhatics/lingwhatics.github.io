Doris and I took a ride to the south shore to follow the route to her future office and then continue to Ikea. Luckily, we arrived at the St-Lambert lock just as they were opening the gate after having the lock open. We explored several paths that I hadn't been on before in the east of Longueuil and into Boucherville.

We ate delicious meatballs for lunch and bought several things including an all wood table. I loaded the box for this table on the back of my cargo bike and we made the trek back home. It was a very nice day for it.
