Another transit strike day with "rush hour" service. We had a show to see at L'Illusion on St-Denis called "Les petites géométries". It was a great show and Fernanda loved it. However, it was at 3pm, so transit couldn't get us there in time but could take us home. I rode with Fernanda on my boke up there. Thankfully, she was able to take the metro home with Doris because it had really started to snow while we were in the show and the ride home was slippery. It took me nearly as long to get down from the Plateau alone as it had to ride up there with a child on my bike.

Total trip distance: 14.87 km

Moving time: 1h15m
