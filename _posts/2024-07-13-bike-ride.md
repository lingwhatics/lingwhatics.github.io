Solo ride to the bout de l'île (eastern point of the island). I went out (and back) along Notre-Dame and took a few detours on the way back. There are plenty of water fountains, washrooms and playgrounds along this route. I even passed a Salvadoran festival being set up in one of the parks.

This was my longest ride and I considered coming back along the north shore, but didn't feel like I had enough snacks for that. Most of the route is all new to me.

Total trip distance: 61.68 km

Moving time: 3h53m
